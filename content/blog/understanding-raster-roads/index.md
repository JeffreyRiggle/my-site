## Setting the foundation

In the last few scene explorations and the update to my home page animation I have been attempting to better understand 3D projection in a 2D space. This is an idea that has been around for quite some time and has been used in many games over the years. That being said most modern games use 3D native abstractions like [WebGL](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API), [Vulkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulkan), [Metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_%28API%29), [Direct3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct3D), and so on.
